# curated_train_270 — summary

**Training subset for S2GO Stage-1 and Stage-2.** Curated from the canonical
nuScenes train split (700 scenes), with **paper-val (150 scenes) excluded** to
preserve a clean train/val split.

Generated by [../../scripts/build_train_val_split.py](../../scripts/build_train_val_split.py),
which wraps [../../scripts/curate_anchor_greedy.py](../../scripts/curate_anchor_greedy.py)
with anchor + greedy submodular curation.

## Headline

| Quantity | Train pool (700) | Curated train (270) | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Scenes | 700 | **270** | 38.6% |
| Keyframes | 28,148 | **10,776** | 38.3% |
| Duration | ~3h 49m | ~1h 28m | — |
| Anchored scenes | — | **33** | — |
| Paper-val overlap | — | **0** | clean |

Note: 270 / 700 = 38.6%, larger fraction than the earlier 270/850 = 31.8% — we
have a smaller pool of candidates here (the 150 val scenes are excluded by
construction), so the same 270-scene budget represents a larger slice of the
available train pool.
